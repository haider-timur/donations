<h1> EASTERN PLAYGROUNDS FUNDRAISER / WHAT YOUR DONATIONS WILL BE USED FOR </h1>

We are fundraising for ourselves (Eastern Playgrounds) and housing support for trans women in Yogyakarta who are facing evictions from their own neighbourhoods.

Our fundraising goal for trans women seeking housing in Jogja is €700. The remaining amount collected will be assessed and allocated in October, dependent on what we need to cover our costs, and on the needs of the broader LGTBQI+ movement. We will share more details then.

<h2> Eastern Playgrounds </h2>
EP is an entirely volunteer-run collective and we sometimes receive minimal contribution/fees for some of the events we do. We use these funds for production, transport, printing, support for folks to attend our events, and other miscellaneous items to create spaces for all of us in the queer asian community. With the upcoming night market, our fund is depleting as the AFK grant only covers part of our costs. Please help us to sustain and continue creating spaces where we all can gather and connect! 

<h2> Financial support for trans women in Jogja </h2>
With the rise of transphobia and homophobia enabled by the state, trans women in Yogyakarta are facing evictions from their own neighbourhoods. One of our EP members is in direct contact with the community in Jogja, and your donations will provide direct financial support to trans women seeking safe housing.

The lives and safety of LGBTQI folks, especially trans women, in Indonesia is increasingly under threat. Fuelled by anti-trans regulation and rhetoric by the government, the rising violence and escalating persecution against the LGBTQI community is partially due to a new presidential regulation identifying LGBTQ culture as a national threat. Please support and stand in solidarity with our fellow queer asian folks by donating!
